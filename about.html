FIFO Group

We Proudly Providing Services Since Past 10 Years...

We at FIFO believe in providing products of the highest quality to ensure loyalty and long term customer satisfaction. FIFO will continually improve its processes and systems by involving everyone in the organization and establishing quality objectives, taking into consideration customer requirements and future plans of the organization.

We have a strong hand in manufacturing past 10 years of all types of Injection Mould like 2 Plate Moulds, 3 Plate Mould, Unscrewing Mould, Hot Runner Mould etc.
